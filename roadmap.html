Roadmap

What ships first. What comes next.

The first release turns your moodboard into a design system and brand guideline. After that, we want to show your brand on more design types.

[01]First release

In the first release.

v1

Moodboard board

Images, swatches, fonts, notes, guided options and Figma import. Mark items "avoid".

v1

Full system generation

8 token categories: color, type, spacing, shape, motion, surface, elevation and copy voice.

v1

Compare and lock

Compare color schemes, font pairs and spacing. Lock the values you like.

v1

Know why

Each value shows its source: your image, your words, your options or your edit.

v1

Live preview

Component gallery and 3 sample pages, in light and dark, at mobile, tablet and desktop width.

v1

Normal or Expressive

One switch adds gradients, glass, grain and playful motion.

v1

Contrast checks

Required text pairs pass WCAG AA before you can export.

v1

Export

DTCG tokens, HTML/CSS/JS code, a DESIGN.md guideline and a docs page.
