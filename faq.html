Product

What is RONA?
RONA is a web app that makes a design system and a brand guideline from your moodboard, your words and your options.
Who is it for?
It is for product owners and founders who want a consistent brand with no manual design work. It is also for designers and developers who want to skip repetitive setup.
Do I need design skills?
No. You can start with images, words or a list of options. The app sets the values and checks contrast. Designers can use the Advanced view to edit each token.
What can I put on the board?
Images, color swatches, text notes, font samples and options. You can also import a Figma file.
I have no moodboard. Can I still use it?
Yes. Use the guided options: style, vibe, theme, emotion, density, corners, mode and brand color.
Can I change the result?
Yes. Select other color schemes and font pairs, move sliders, or edit each value. A value that you change is locked, so a new result does not overwrite it.
Why did the app select this color or this font?
Each value shows its source: from an image, from your text, from an option or from your edit. The Advanced view also shows the method and the confidence.

Preview and export

What can I preview?
At launch: a component gallery and 3 sample pages (landing page, dashboard and article), in light and dark mode and at mobile, tablet and desktop width. Later: ecommerce store, pitch deck, brand manual, social post, product mockup and bumper video previews are on our roadmap.
What do I get when I export?
One zip with design tokens (DTCG JSON), a ready HTML/CSS/JS codebase, a guideline file (DESIGN.md) and an HTML docs page.
Does the export include ecommerce themes, decks or videos?
No. The export is the design system and the brand guideline. Use them as the baseline for your other designs.
Does it work with Tailwind, SCSS or Figma variables?
Not directly in the first release. The export uses the DTCG standard. You can convert DTCG files with your own tools.
Does it work with AI coding tools?
The guideline file (DESIGN.md) has a part for AI coding agents. It gives exact token names, class names and short rules.
Are the fonts included?
No. The export never includes font files. It gives each font's name, source, license and the steps to add it.
Is the result accessible?
The app checks each required text and background pair against WCAG AA. The export stops until the required pairs pass. The code also supports reduced motion, reduced transparency and forced colors.

Privacy and AI

Where is my data?
You can start with no account. Your work then stays as one draft in your browser. To save your projects and open them on any device, sign in with a free account: your projects and their images are saved on our server. We never use saved projects to train our models. You can also download a project file, and delete a project or your account at any time.
Does it use AI?
Yes, in two ways. Small models read your images in your browser, and rules make your design system. On paid plans the rules run on our server, which does not keep your request or the result. It keeps only a usage count. An AI language model runs on a server only when you ask ("Describe more", "Write description", "Write examples") or when the rules are not sure. The app tells you before it sends an image.
Do you train on my designs?
Only if you choose to. "Share for training" is opt-in, with a consent screen. You can delete a shared board later.
