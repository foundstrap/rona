Seven groups, one system.

01

Your board

Start from what you already have: images, colors, fonts and words.

  • One board

    Pan and zoom. Add, move and resize images, swatches, notes, fonts and option chips.

  • Eyedropper

    Pick a color from an image on the board.

  • Style summary

    A short text that describes your style, for example "Warm, muted palette. Soft corners."

  • Describe more

    Ask AI to read an image in more detail. RONA will tell you before it sends the image.

  • Guided options

    No moodboard? Pick style, vibe, theme, emotion, density, corners, mode and brand color.

  • Figma import

    Paste a Figma link. Styles from Figma come in locked.

02

Your system

8 token categories, built from one style profile.

  • Color

    Palettes from color theory and classic color combinations. 11-step scales. Light and dark mode.

  • Typography

    Curated open-license font pairs. A fluid type scale for mobile and desktop.

  • Spacing

    A scale from your density. Touch targets of 44 px or more on mobile.

  • Shape and motion

    Corners, borders and sizes. Durations and easing. Reduced-motion settings always win.

  • Surface and elevation

    Flat, gradient, glass or textured surfaces. Shadow levels in your palette.

  • Copy voice

    Tone for errors, success and empty states. Words to use and words to avoid.

03

Compare and lock

Keep control of every value.

  • Candidates

    3 to 5 color schemes, and the top 3 font pairs and spacing options.

  • Combination library

    Browse classic color combinations, sorted by how close they are to your colors.

  • Regenerate a section

    Make one section again. Locked values stay.

  • Locks and reset

    Unlock one value, one section or all. Go back to the generated value at any time.

  • Undo and redo

    For all edits in a session.

  • Normal or Expressive

    One switch adds gradients, glass, grain and playful motion.

04

Live preview

See each choice on real layouts before you export.

  • Component gallery

    Buttons, inputs, cards, badges, modals, navigation and more.

  • 3 sample pages

    A landing page, a dashboard and an article.

  • Modes and widths

    Light, dark and side by side, at mobile, tablet and desktop width.

  • Accessibility checks

    Contrast, reduced motion and reduced transparency, in the preview.

05

Export

One zip with the parts you select.

  • Design tokens

    DTCG JSON files for base, light, dark, effects and density modes.

  • Ready code

    Static HTML, CSS and JS. Open it in a browser. No build step.

  • Brand guideline

    A DESIGN.md file for your team, with a part for AI coding tools. It follows the open DESIGN.md format.

  • Docs page and font guide

    One HTML page with live samples, and a guide to each font and its license.

  • Project file

    Download your full project as a file. Needs a free account.

06

Privacy and access

Your work stays yours.

  • Cloud save

    Sign in with a free account to save your projects and open them on any device. With no account, you keep one draft in your browser.

  • AI only when needed

    Small models read your images in the browser. A language model runs only for some tasks.

  • Free to start

    Edit, preview and export with no account. Generation from your board is a premium feature.

  • Accessible editor

    The editor targets WCAG 2.2 AA, with keyboard control and a screen reader list view.

  • Open-license fonts

    The font picker shows open-license fonts. An unknown license gets a warning.

  • Share for training

    Opt-in only, with a consent screen. You can delete a shared board later.
