[07]Motion

Motion

Short and sleek. Tiles rise 16 px and fade in on scroll. Hover moves an arrow or adds a glow. When reduced motion is on, all of this stops.

Fast · 140 ms

Hover color, press.

Base · 240 ms

Glow, arrow move, accordion.

Slow · 520 ms

Tile reveal, hero sequence (GSAP).
